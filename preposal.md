## What idea(s) do you have for your final project?

1. Trolley problem game: Making a light-novel/SIM style game that involves going through levels of the trolley problem with it getting harder to make a decision for each one. There's no way to win, but it could be a troll game that tells you messsages like "Congratulations, you're a horrible person" or something like that
2. Conti-Kombat: A combination of Geoguesser and Mortal Kombat. Two players select one of four continent (Europe, Africa, Asia, South America) and randomly a country will be highlighted for each player. If they type in the right name for a country they can attack their opponent, and whoever reaches 5 in a row first can deliver a finisher. This one might be the most workshoppy
3. How Many Kids Can you Beat?: One of those never ending games (think Subway Surfers) where you just want a high score. Kids will surround you in a circle, and they pop out one at a time from any side to rush you, and the more time passes, the more kids come out at a time and the faster they get. Although you can start to get power ups like a belt, or a chair to help you, but the game ends once the kids take your hp bar all the way down. High Score will be based on amount of kids defeated, not time lasted.
